IGMASK

PUBLIC PROFILE FINDER

Find public Instagram profiles from image clues

Upload an image. IG MASK reads visible text such as @usernames, watermarks and public labels, then creates search links. It does not identify people by face.

Detected clues

How it works

  1. OCR reads visible text from the uploaded image.
  2. IG MASK extracts public-looking @usernames and useful text clues.
  3. Search links open public web/Instagram search pages so you can verify the result yourself.

Privacy: the image is processed locally in your browser by default. No face recognition or private-account discovery is performed.
